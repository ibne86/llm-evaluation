SQuAD 2.0 (2018), Pranav Rajpurkar, Robin Jia, and Percy Liang.
Official source: https://rajpurkar.github.io/SQuAD-explorer/
Dataset card: https://huggingface.co/datasets/rajpurkar/squad_v2
Paper: Know What You Don't Know: Unanswerable Questions for SQuAD
https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.03822

The SQuAD portions of pilot.json are a selected subset of the publisher's
dev-v2.0.json. Contexts and questions are preserved without truncation.
Changes: seeded selection, category labels, deduplicated reference aliases,
an explicit abstention expectation, and a wrapper with provenance metadata.
Original identifiers and original answer spans are retained. Source plausible
answers for impossible questions are provenance only, never reference answers.

The two project-authored custom fixtures are also offered under CC BY-SA 4.0
so the distributed pilot dataset has a consistent data license. Preserve this
notice, source attribution, license link, and modification history when sharing
this dataset or adapted dataset material. No source-author endorsement is implied.
